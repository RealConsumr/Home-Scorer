About Our Data

🏫 Schools, Parks & Libraries

School, park, and library locations come from the Utah SGID (Statewide Geospatial Information Database), the official statewide geographic data repository. This data includes public schools, recreation areas, and public library facilities across Salt Lake and Davis counties.

Data updates: Regularly maintained by the State of Utah
Link: Utah SGID Open Data Portal

❤️ Life Expectancy

Life expectancy data by census tract comes from the CDC's USALEEP (U.S. Small-area Life Expectancy Estimates Program). This dataset provides life expectancy estimates at the census tract level (approximately neighborhood-sized areas) based on mortality data and demographic factors.

Data year: 2018-2022
Link: CDC USALEEP

💨 Air Quality

Air quality data (PM2.5 fine particulate matter) comes from the EPA's AirData monitoring network. PM2.5 readings are from official monitoring stations throughout the Salt Lake Valley, showing annual average concentrations of fine particulates that can affect respiratory health.

Data year: 2024
Link: EPA AirData

🗳️ Voter Trends

Precinct-level 2024 U.S. Presidential election results from official Salt Lake County and Davis County Statements of Votes Cast. Shows Trump vote percentage by precinct, enabling fine-grained analysis of political leanings in specific neighborhoods. Score reflects how conservative or progressive a precinct is.

Data year: 2024 General Election
Source: Official county election departments

🌳 Tree Density

Tree canopy cover comes from the NLCD (National Land Cover Database), a comprehensive satellite-based dataset showing percent tree cover across the United States. Higher tree density indicates more forest canopy, which correlates with urban cooling, air quality, and quality of life.

Data year: 2025
Link: USGS NLCD

🛒 Food Access

Food access indicators come from the USDA Food Access Research Atlas, measuring distance to the nearest supermarket and prevalence of food deserts. This helps identify neighborhoods with limited access to healthy, affordable food options.

Data year: 2024
Link: USDA Food Access Research Atlas

🚗 Commute Time

Commute times are calculated using the OSRM (Open Source Routing Machine), an open-source routing engine based on OpenStreetMap data. Distances represent driving time from your home address to our benchmark work location (10 Exchange Pl, Salt Lake City).

Method: Road network routing
Link: Open Source Routing Machine

📍 Precinct Boundaries

Voting precinct boundaries come from the New York Times 2024 Precinct Map Project, which compiled official precinct data from election officials nationwide. This enables precise geographic matching of addresses to their voting precincts.

Data year: 2024
Link: NYT Precinct Map Project

Data Privacy: All addresses and calculations are processed client-side in your browser. No data is sent to servers or stored.

Methodology: The Home Scorer uses weighted scoring across nine categories. You can customize weights using the "Customize Weights" button above. Scores range from 0-100, with 80+ being Excellent, 60-79 Good, 40-59 Fair, and below 40 Poor.
